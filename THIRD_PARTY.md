# Third-party data, models and attribution

This repository contains **code and aggregate results only**. Raw data, derived per-patient tables and text embeddings are not redistributed (see `.gitignore`); `scripts/` and `data/manifest.json` download and verify them from the original sources.

| Component | Source | License / terms | Use here |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pathology report text | TCGA-Reports — Kefeli J, Tatonetti N. *TCGA-Reports: A machine-readable pathology report resource for benchmarking text-based AI models.* Patterns 5(3):100933 (2024). Data: Mendeley Data DOI 10.17632/hyg5xkznpx.1; code: github.com/jkefeli/tcga-path-reports | Data CC BY 4.0; code MIT | BRCA reports embedded locally. `results/leakage_scan.json` quotes a few short excerpts for the leakage audit, with attribution under CC BY 4.0. |
| Gene expression | UCSC Xena, `TCGA.BRCA.sampleMap/HiSeqV2` (TCGA Research Network data) | Xena metadata: public, redistribution allowed; TCGA publication guidelines apply | Downloaded at run time; not redistributed. |
| Clinical data and endpoints | TCGA Pan-Cancer Clinical Data Resource — Liu J, et al. *An Integrated TCGA Pan-Cancer Clinical Data Resource to Drive High-Quality Survival Outcome Analytics.* Cell 173(2):400–416 (2018) | GDC open-access publication data | Downloaded at run time; not redistributed. |
| Text encoder | `microsoft/BiomedNLP-BiomedBERT-base-uncased-abstract-fulltext` (pinned revision in `results/text_embedding.json`) | MIT | Frozen, inference only; weights not redistributed. |

The results shown here are based upon data generated by the TCGA Research Network: https://www.cancer.gov/tcga.

Research and education only. This is not a clinical tool and has no clinical validation.
